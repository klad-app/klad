# Filling

Fill line art with flat colour that stays editable and can follow the lines when they change.

## Fill inside lines

1. Add a paint layer under your lines for the colour.
2. Press **G** and set **reference** in the bar over the canvas to the lines layer.
3. Click inside the lines.

If a fill leaks through a small opening, raise **gap**. If paper shows between the line and the colour, raise **grow**.

## Fill a shape you draw

With **G**, drag a loop instead of clicking. The inside of the loop is filled.

## Fill again after the lines changed

Press **cmd K** and run **refill this drawing**. **show gaps** outlines the areas no fill covers yet.

## Colour every frame of an animation

1. Fill the first drawing.
2. In the timeline, select it and the frames after it.
3. Click **fill like the first drawing** in the selection bar. klad finds each area in the other drawings by its shape.
