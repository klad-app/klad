# Shapes, paths and text

Draw shapes and text that stay sharp at any size and keep their points editable. They go on a vector layer: click **vector** in the Layers pane first.

## Draw a rectangle or an ellipse

Press **U** and drag. Hold **shift** for a square or a circle.

## Draw a path

Press **P**, click to place corners and drag to pull out curves. Click the first point to close the path, or press **Return** to leave it open.

## Change the points

Press **A**, click the shape and drag its points and handles. Alt click on the outline adds a point.

To move several points together, drag a box around them, then drag one of them. Hold **shift** while you drag the box to take the points of every shape on the layer, not only the one you clicked.

## Fill a shape with a gradient

Select the shape with **V**, then in layer settings switch its fill to **linear** or **radial** and edit the stops.

## Write text

Press **T**, click and type, and press **Esc** to finish. The font picker also finds any family on Google Fonts, and the font is kept inside the drawing.

## Repeat a shape

Select it and pick **repeat it**, **scatter it** or **mirror it** from the Layer menu. The copies stay settings you can change.
